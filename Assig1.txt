Three popular websites that use React their  one "reusable" part are follows :


1-Facebook -The ChatBox component - Each instance of the component receives different Props (the friend’s name, their profile picture, and the message history). The "State" handles whether the window is minimized or popped up.
2-Instagram - The images, like and share components are these components comes together to form a single post. 
3-Netflix - The MovieRow component - The Row component is a container that takes a "Title" (like "Trending Now") and an "Array of Data" (the movies). It then maps through that data to generate the individual movie cards.
